The 5 Core Picks

01 Top Pick

Coffee Bar Caddy

  • Best for: Cups, lids and sweeteners that are spread across the counter.
  • Why it works: A divided tray keeps everything grouped in one spot that's easy to lift and wipe around.
  • Check before you buy: Your counter depth, the caddy size and compartment sizes.
  • Skip it if: You already own a tray that fits beside your machine.
Find a Caddy That Fits
02 Space Saver

Compact Mug Tree

  • Best for: Small counters where mugs are eating up prep space.
  • Why it works: Everyday mugs stay within reach while the counter stays clear.
  • Check before you buy: Base stability, hook spacing and clearance under your cabinets.
  • Skip it if: Your mugs live in a cabinet right above the machine.
Compare Compact Mug Trees
03 Matching Pick

Glass Syrup Dispensers

  • Best for: Daily syrup drinkers who want one tidy, matching row.
  • Why it works: Clear bottles with pumps and simple labels make flavors easy to grab and refill.
  • Check before you buy: Pump fit, bottle height under your cabinets and cleaning instructions.
  • Skip it if: You drink your coffee black.
Pick Matching Dispensers
04 Optional Upgrade

An Espresso Machine That Fits Your Counter

  • Best for: Turning a styled corner into a working latte station.
  • Three to compare: The HiBREW H10B Lite is a compact, budget-friendly start with 20-bar pressure and a milk frother. The Breville Bambino has one of the smallest footprints. The Breville Barista Express adds a built-in grinder but needs more height and depth.
  • Check before you buy: Width, depth, height under cabinets (with room to fill the tank), steam wand clearance and a nearby outlet.
  • Skip it if: You mostly drink drip coffee or your counter can't spare the depth.
Check the HiBREW H10B Lite Compare All Three Machines
05 No Counter Space

Astergrain Coffee Bar Cabinet With Fridge Space

  • Best for: A coffee-by-day, wine-by-night bar in a dining nook or living room.
  • Why it works: Wine glass racks, a fluted sliding door, a built-in charging station for your machine and an open bay for a mini fridge (19.69" W x 19.29" D x 33.27" H).
  • Drawback to know: It's 42.5 inches wide and needs assembly. Measure your wall and your mini fridge first.
  • Skip it if: You only have counter space, not wall space.
