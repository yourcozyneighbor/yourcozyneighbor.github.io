    Start Here: Pick Your Kitchen Spot

    Cabinet doorsTie on velvet bows. Use removable hooks for doors without handles. Kitchen windowHang lightweight garland on a tension rod and weave in timer lights. Open shelvesTuck a short garland behind a jar or cookbook. Kitchen islandGroup mini trees and LED tapers on a tray you already own.

    The 7 Picks

    01 Top Pick

    Velvet Christmas Bows With Wired Tails

    • Best for: Cabinets with handles you can tie bows around.
    • Why it works: Long tails tie around handles, and wired edges help the bows hold their shape.
    • Check before you buy: Count your handles before choosing a pack. Keep velvet away from the stove and mixer.
    See Velvet Bow Sets
    02 Handle-Free Cabinets

    Clear Removable Adhesive Hooks

    • Best for: Hanging bows on cabinet doors without handles.
    • Why it works: Small clear hooks give the bows a place to hang without drilling.
    • Check before you buy: Confirm the finish is compatible, test a hidden spot and follow the package removal directions.
    See Removable Hooks
    03 Window Setup

    Short Adjustable Tension Rod

    • Best for: Hanging garland inside a window frame or between two solid surfaces.
    • Why it works: The rod stays in place by pressure and comes down when the season ends.
    • Check before you buy: Measure the inside width and check the rod's load limit before adding greenery.
    Compare Tension Rod Sizes
    04 Window or Shelf

    Lightweight Faux Pine Garland

    • Best for: Dressing a tension rod or an open shelf.
    • Why it works: Lightweight greenery can wrap around the rod or tuck behind items on a shelf.
    • Check before you buy: Check length and weight for the opening. Heavy garland can make a tension rod slip.
    See Faux Pine Garland
    05 Cord-Free Glow

    Battery Fairy Lights With Timer

    • Best for: Adding glow to a window garland or island tray.
    • Why it works: A battery pack keeps the setup cord-free, and the timer handles the evening switch-on.
    • Check before you buy: Check which batteries it takes and where the battery pack will sit.
    Find Timer Fairy Lights
    06 Island or Sill

    Mini Bottle Brush Trees

    • Best for: Adding height to a small island tray or windowsill.
    • Why it works: A few heights make a compact arrangement feel finished.
    • Check before you buy: Check the base widths so they fit your tray. Some sets shed bristles.
    Shop Mini Tree Sets
    07 Island Glow

    Flameless LED Taper Candles

    • Best for: Adding candlelight to an island centerpiece.
    • Why it works: LED tapers give the tray a candlelit look without an open flame.
    • Check before you buy: Confirm the taper diameter fits your holders and check the battery requirements.
    See LED Taper Candles

    Start with two: Velvet bows and one strand of timer lights are enough for a small kitchen. Keep fabric and greenery away from the stove.
