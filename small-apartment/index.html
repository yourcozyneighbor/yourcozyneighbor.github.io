    The 5 Picks

    01 Top Pick

    HOMFINE Washable Modern Area Rug

    • Best for: Defining a living area in a studio or open room.
    • Why it works: Machine-washable with geometric styles in several sizes.
    • Check before you buy: Size (front legs of the sofa should sit on it) and washer capacity.
    • Skip it if: You need a thick, plush pile.
    See the Washable Rug
    02 Cozy Layer

    HOMFINE Fluffy Faux Fur Throw

    • Best for: Making a plain sofa or bed look styled.
    • Why it works: Bubble texture adds softness in one easy layer.
    • Check before you buy: Color and size (50x60 and up).
    • Skip it if: You prefer flat-weave throws.
    See the Faux Fur Throw
    03 Hidden Storage

    HAKELPOK Foldable Wicker Cube Baskets (4 Pack)

    • Best for: Cube shelves full of visible clutter.
    • Why it works: Warm wicker texture hides cables, mail and remotes. They fold flat when not in use.
    • Check before you buy: Your cube size (these are 12 in).
    • Skip it if: Your shelves are 11 in or less.
    See the Cube Baskets
    04 No Wiring

    Dimmable Plug-In Wall Sconces (Set of 2)

    • Best for: Dark corners and bedside light without a hardwired fixture.
    • Why it works: Fabric shades with a plug-in cord and dimmer give soft, layered light.
    • Check before you buy: Cord length to your outlet and whether you can use removable hooks.
    • Skip it if: Your lease bans any wall mounting.
    See the Plug-In Sconces
    05 Space Trick

    DUMOS Arched Full-Length Mirror With Stand

    • Best for: Bouncing light around a small room.
    • Why it works: Freestanding, so there is no drilling. The arch reads more high-end than a rectangle.
    • Check before you buy: Height and the floor space the stand needs.
    • Skip it if: You have no free wall or corner.
