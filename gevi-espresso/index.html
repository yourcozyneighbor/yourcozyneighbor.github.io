    The 4 Setups

    01 Top Pick

    Gevi Dual Boiler Espresso Machine With Grinder (ECMF0)

    • Best for: Latte and cappuccino drinkers who make more than one drink at a time.
    • Why it works: Grinder, brewer and milk frother in one machine. The double boiler brews and steams at once, so back-to-back lattes feel easy.
    • Price check: $339.99 on gevi.com as of Nov 24 (listed regular price $449.99).
    • Drawback to know: The biggest pick here: 27.9 pounds, 16.9 inches deep, and a 1-liter tank you'll refill more often.
    • Skip it if: Your counter is shallower than 18 inches or you rarely drink milk-based coffee.
    Check the Dual Boiler Price
    02 Best Value

    Gevi 20 Bar Espresso Machine With Built-In Grinder (ECMI0)

    • Best for: Fresh-ground espresso without a second appliance on the counter.
    • Why it works: Beans in the top, espresso out the bottom: 30 grind settings, PID temperature control, a 58mm portafilter, steam wand and hot water, all 11.5 inches wide.
    • Price check: $275.99 on gevi.com as of Nov 24 (listed regular price $349.99), with a 2.3-liter tank.
    • Drawback to know: Built-in grinders are noisy, and at 16.5 inches it's the tallest pick. Check your cabinet clearance.
    • Skip it if: You make coffee while others are still sleeping.
    See the Built-In Grinder Model
    03 For Learners

    Gevi 20 Bar Semi-Automatic With OPV (ECMN0) + Gevi Burr Grinder

    • Best for: Beginners who want to learn what makes a good shot.
    • Why it works: The touch display shows pressure, time and temperature as you brew, and the OPV valve holds pressure at 9 to 12 bar. Pair it with the 35-setting burr grinder.
    • Price check: Machine $271.99 + grinder $69.99 on gevi.com as of Nov 24, so about $341.98 together.
    • Drawback to know: Two appliances and two plugs. The grinder adds about 4.7 inches of width.
    • Skip it if: You want one-touch coffee. The built-in grinder model is simpler.
    See the Semi-Auto Machine See the Burr Grinder
    04 Smallest Footprint

    Gevi Espresso Barista Starter Set With Steam Wand & Grinder

    • Best for: Narrow counters, first-time espresso makers and gift-givers.
    • Why it works: A compact machine just 5.5 inches wide and 12 inches tall, with pre-infusion and a pressure gauge, plus the same 35-setting burr grinder.
    • Price check: $189.98 for the set on gevi.com as of Nov 24 (listed regular price $299.98).
    • Drawback to know: 51mm portafilter instead of 58mm and a 34-ounce tank, so it's built for smaller batches.
    • Skip it if: You host often and make several milk drinks in a row.
